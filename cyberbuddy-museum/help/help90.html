Feel Good Messages

Isn't it nice when someone gives you a compliment or a pat on the back for a job well done? Wouldn't it be nice to have someone around to feed you compliments. Let CyberBuddy make your day. This is especially great if you have small children using the computer. You can have special messages that surprise them and give them a boost.
The "Get" button will play one of your messages now.
Checking the box marked "Feel Good Messages" will allow CyberBuddy to give you compliments every now and again.
To add or change the Feel Good Messages, click the "Feel Good Messages" button on the "News" tab of CyberBuddy or go to the Custom Message section.
