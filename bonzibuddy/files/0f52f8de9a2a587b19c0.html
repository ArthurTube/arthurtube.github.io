Admission requirements for international students

Thank you for your interest in University of Phoenix Online. Please note that, because you'll earn your degree entirely online, your program will not involve U.S. residency.

To qualify for undergraduate admission, applicants outside the U.S and Canada must:

  • Hold a high school diploma or equivalent
  • Be 21 years of age or older
  • Score a minimum of 550 on the paper-based exam or 213 on the computer-based exam of the Test of English as a Foreign Language, TOEFL (non-native speakers of English only)
  • Be employed upon enrollment, or have access to an organizational environment
