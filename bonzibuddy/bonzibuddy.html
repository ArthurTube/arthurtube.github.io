BonziMAIL© Help

 


We are in the process of working to make the BonziMAIL feature even better, but in the meantime there are a small number of our customers that are having troubles sending BonziMAIL messages.  If you are having trouble sending BonziMAIL messages, please look through this short list of possible solutions to see if we can help you.

Before you continue...

Before moving on to the rest of this page, please verify your version of BonziBUDDY. Right click on Bonzi's tummy and go to My Options > Settings. At the top of the window, in the colored title bar, you should see your BonziBUDDY version number. If you do not see a version number or if it is not 3.0.8 or higher, please visit the BonziBUDDY Update Page and download the BonziBUDDY update file. Make sure that the version number changes to 3.0.8 or higher after running the update and restarting your computer.

NETZERO, COMPUSERVE, and AT&T USERS, PLEASE NOTE: 

Bonzi-MAIL is not compatible with NetZero or CompuServe at this time. Were sorry; if you use NetZero or CompuServe as your ISP, you wont be able to send Bonzi-MAIL messages due to the way their services are configured. If you use AT&T Worldnet as your ISP, you will need to contact their technical support staff and ask them to "allow you to use Port 25 when you are connected to the Internet." They will know what this means if you inform them that BonziBUDDY's BonziMAIL feature must be able to use Port 25 to work correctly.

BonziMAIL Support Issues

  • My Bonzi-Mail messages are not being sent.

First and foremost, make sure that both you and your recipient have the latest version of BonziBUDDY. Only the latest version will work properly with BonziMAIL. Just visit http://www.bonzi.com and click on the link to BonziBUDDY to download the latest version of the software. Keeping your BonziBUDDY updated on a regular basis will ensure that you have all of the latest features and animations installed.

When installing BonziBUDDY, make sure that you disable any Norton Anti-Virus software or Norton Crash Guard software. These programs prevent key files needed by BonziMAIL from registering properly during installation and effectively render BonziMAIL useless. You can disable or exit these programs by right-clicking on their system tray icons (next to the time on the right-hand side of your Start toolbar). Make sure you restart your computer after the BonziBUDDY installation is complete to turn these programs back on.

When creating your BonziMAIL messages, drag the animations in the right hand column into your messages and Bonzi will display those animations while he reads your message to the recipient. IMPORTANT: Please make sure that you do not resize the animation images in the text window of the message. Resizing the images of Bonzis animations will stop the animation from happening when Bonzi reads the message to the recipient.

  • When I try to send a message, Bonzi tells me that my EMSMTP.dll file is missing.

This problem is usually caused when BonziBUDDY was installed while certain virus protect or crash guard programs were running. It is always good to disable programs like Norton Anti-Virus, Norton Crash Guard, or McAfee Virus Scan when you install any kind of software. If you are getting this error, reinstall the BonziBUDDY files with these kinds of programs disabled.

First, uninstall BonziBUDDY by going to Start > Programs > BonziBUDDY > Remove BonziBUDDY. Once he is gone, restart your computer and when it is restarted, disable the scanning program(s). You can usually do this by right-clicking on the system tray icon for the program next to your clock in the lower right-hand corner of your screen and choosing exit or disable. Once the program is disabled, log on to the Internet and go to http://www.bonzi.com and install the BonziBUDDY again.

IMPORTANT: Make sure that you dont check your Email or run any email file attachments while your virus scanner is not running, as this increases your likelihood of getting e-mailed a computer virus.

Once the install of BonziBUDDY is complete, restart your computer to finalize the BonziBUDDY install and turn your virus scanner or crash guard program back on. This should fix the problem you were having with BonziMAIL.

  • How can I open and play my Bonzi-Mail messages? Do my recipients need to have BonziBuddy installed?

When receiving a message through BonziMAIL, just open the file attached to the message to start BonziBUDDY and have him read the message. You may want to uncheck the box at the bottom of the Open/Save As window that says Always ask for this type of file. By un-checking this box you will not be asked if you want to open the file or save it. Just turn off that check box and choose Open and from then on the BonziMAIL messages will always be opened when you select them.

BonziMAIL works fine on Web-based Email services like Yahoo, Hotmail, and Excite! Just choose to Open the message attachment from its current location and BonziBuddy will read the message to you while performing the embedded animations.

Your recipients will need to have the latest version of BonziBuddy installed on their computer in order to view your Bonzi-MAIL message. When your recipients receive their Bonzi-MAIL messages, they will also receive instructions for downloading the latest version of BonziBuddy.
