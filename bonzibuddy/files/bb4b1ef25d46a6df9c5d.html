Answers:

  1. What is the BonziBUDDY™ Gold Membership?

Membership in the Gold program gives you access to all of the BonziBUDDY™ add-on modules and Priority technical support.  Without any additional fees, your monthly or yearly membership in the BonziBUDDY™ Gold Program will entitle you to all existing BonziBUDDY add-on modules including the Talking Module and the E-Mail Reader module.  You will also have access to any new add-on modules that are developed while you're a member.  In addition to this, you will have access to a Priority Technical Support form that you can use to send in questions and get help with the program.  Your messages will be answered first, before any messages from non-Gold users of BONZI Software products!

  1. How much is the BonziBUDDY Gold Membership?

The BonziBUDDY Gold Membership bills one time at $49.00.  There is no recurring billing and one payment entitles you to all current and future add-on modules that have been or will be released for BonziBUDDY!

  1. What kind of guarantee do you offer with the BonziBUDDY Gold Membership?

We offer a full 60-day guarantee! If you are not happy with your purchase, just contact us using any of the contact methods on your sales invoice and we'll be happy to process a full refund for you. Refunds are not processed past our 60-day guarantee.

  1. How do I know if my Gold Membership is properly installed?

To know if your Gold Membership is properly installed, just right-click once on Bonzi's belly to open his menu and choose "Main Menu."  Then, click on the wooden Check For New Add-Ons sign in the upper right corner.  You should see a link to download your BonziBUDDY add-on modules.  If you don't see this link, see #5 below.  See the BonziBUDDY™ Gold Membership User's Guide for more information.

  1. What do I do if I paid for my Gold Membership and it is not working properly?

Most problems with the Gold Membership result from incomplete or corrupt downloads.  It is best to run the download and installation of the Gold Membership again if you're having trouble.

If the original download page has been closed and you no longer have access to the download link, you can visit our Email Request page to get another download.  This page requires your credit card number to verify your purchase of the software.  The page uses SSL encryption technology to protect your sensitive data.

If you think it is installed properly but you may not know how to use it, visit the BonziBUDDY™ Gold Membership User's Guide for more information.
