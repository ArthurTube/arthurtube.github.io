Categories of Weather Images Available

  • Satellite Images: Including Infrared, Enhanced Infrared, Visible, Satellite/Radar Composite and Water Vapor. Images are available from the current time to 12 hours in the past in 1 hour increments.
  • Radar Images: Including national and regional mosaics available from the current time to 6 hours in the past in 30 minute increments.
  • Surface Data: Including Daily Precipitation, High Temperature, High Temperature Contour, Low Temperature, Low Temperature Contour, Monthly Precipitation, Snow Cover, Sea Surface Temperature Anomaly, Sea Surface Temperature, Surface Depiction, Surface Fronts, Regional and National Surface Maps. Images are available from the current time to 12 hours in the past in 1 hour increments.
  • Upper Air Data: Including Constant Height Plots and Contours and Mandatory Height Plots. Images are available from the current time to 72 hours in the past in 12 hour increments.
  • Forecast Model Data:
    • RUC: Including the 4 Panel and Initial plots.
    • ECMWF: Including the 3 day, 4 day, 5 day, 6 day and 4 Panel Plots for North America and Europe.
    • GFS: Including; the 4 Panel, 300mb, 400mb, 500mb, 700mb, 850mb, 1000mb, Pressure/Precipitation and Relative Humidity plots for the current time to 5 days in the future in 12 hour increments.
    • GFSx: Including; the 4 Panel, 300mb, 400mb, 500mb, 700mb, 850mb, 1000mb, Pressure/Precipitation, Height/SLP and Relative Humidity plots for the current time to 10 days in the future in 12 hour increments.
    • NAM: Including the 4 Panel, 300mb, 400mb, 500mb, 700mb, 850mb, 1000mb, Pressure/Precipitation, Surface and Relative Humidity plots for the current time to 60 hours in the future in 12 hour increments.
    • MOS: Including the GFS MOS, MOS Depiction and the NAM MOS plots. Forecast plots are available for the current time through 60 hours in the future for the NAM model and 8 days in the future for the GFS model in 12 hour increments.
