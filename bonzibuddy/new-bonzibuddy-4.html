What is "BONZI and the Treasure Chest!" for BonziBUDDY?

The story, 'Bonzi and the Treasure Chest' is a story of friendship.  BONZI and his friends discover a treasure chest along the sea shore.   They use the riches from the treasure chest to help celebrate Effie's birthday party.  This story was designed to emphasize the importance of friendship and sharing!  Come along with BONZI and his friends as they continue to explore and learn more about each other and the world they live in.

Best of all, my new Storybook has all kinds of new features:

  • Print Feature - You can now print a color or black and white copy of the entire book. This will allow you to tale "BONZI and the Treasure Chest" with you on trips, to school, birthday parties and even to bed!
  • Built in Coloring Book -  Do you know someone who likes to color?  My new storybook also comes with built in coloring book.   You can now print each page in coloring book format time and time again. This is one coloring book that you will never have to replace!
  • Create your own Treasure Chest - You can even build your very own treasure chest with your name on it! I've made it simple to print out and color. All you'll need is some scissors and tape!

What is the "Storybook Reader" Module for BonziBUDDY?

The "Storybook Reader" add-on module allows you and I to read stories together. I've designed several settings with my storybook software:

  • I read the entire story to you. (Recommended for ages 2 and above.)

  • We read together. (Recommended for ages 4 and above.)

  • You read to me. (Recommended for ages 5 to 6 and above.)
