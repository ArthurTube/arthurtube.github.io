NAM Surface Forecasts


Current NAM Surface Forecast

The surface chart shows near surface weather conditions and is intended to aid in severe weather location. The parameters plotted are 2 meter above ground temperature in Celsius (in color contours), convergence (black lines, interval=2, shaded > 0), 2 meter above ground dewpoints in Celsius (colored lines, interval=5) and winds plotted as vectors
