Installing

  1. The videos I watch are choppy or the radio stations I listen to cut in or out.  What can I do?

    Streaming media is very sensitive to slower connections (modem connections) and Internet traffic.  Any combination of noisy phone lines, busy Internet service providers, Internet network congestion, or busy media hosting web site will cause this kind of problem.  There is usually not much you can do to fix this short of moving up to DSL or Cable Internet access to increase your speed.  The power and speed of your computer's processor and amount of RAM (memory) have a part as well, although it is a much smaller part than the speed and quality of your Internet connection.

  2. When I click on links in the Entertainment center window, they open up very small.  Why?

    These windows are controlled by the programmers that work on the Windows Media web site and are not in the control of BONZI Software.  You can maximize any of the windows you would like to get a better look at by clicking the square box (maximize) button in the top right corner of the window.

  3. What does Broadband mean and should I use that section of the Windows Media web site?

    The term "broadband" refers to the methods of high-speed access to the Internet like cable, DSL, or T1 connections.  This section of the Windows Media web site houses high quality video and audio streams that must have an Internet connection of 100 kbps or better.  This section is only recommended for users of DSL, Cable, T1, and faster Internet connections.
