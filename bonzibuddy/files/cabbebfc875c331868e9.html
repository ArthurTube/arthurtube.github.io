Answers:

  1. Why can't I hear BonziBUDDY talk to me?

    Please check to make sure your "Use only Preferred Devices" box is checked.  To check, please click Start, Settings, Control Panel, and then double click on the Multimedia option.  Next, click on the Audio tab and make sure the "Use only Preferred Devices" box is checked.

    If this doesn't fix the problem (or if BonziBUDDY's voice isn't clear and audible), you may need to re-install your sound card drivers or the software that comes with your sound card.

  2. When I click on the "Talk to Me" option, it reads "Speech Input is not available in English."  What does that mean?

    After downloading and installing the Add-On module, you should see a list of words BonziBUDDY will recognize when you click the "Talk to Me" option.  If you don't, please try the following:

    Please uninstall the Talking Add-On for BonziBUDDY.  To do this, please click Start, Settings, Control Panel, double click on the Add/Remove Programs icon and remove the Microsoft Speech Recognition Engine 4.0 (English).   If you don't see the Microsoft Speech Recognition Engine, please go to the next step.

    From your desktop, open My Computer > C: Drive > Program Files > BonziBUDDY. Once you have the BonziBUDDY folder open, run the ACTCNC file by double clicking on it. This will reinstall Microsoft’s Speech Recognition software.  (If the ACTCNC file is not there, please redownload the Talking Add-On file (BB1SETUP.EXE) by clicking here.

    Once you've reinstalled the ACTCNC file or re-downloaded the Talking Add-On module, please click the "Talk to Me" option.  You should now see the different words and phrases that BonziBUDDY recognizes.

  3. When I talk to BonziBUDDY, why doesn't he recognize what I'm saying?

    First, please verify that BonziBUDDY is set up correctly. Right-click on BonziBUDDY until his jungle window comes up. Choose the "Interact with Me" tab and select "Talk to Me." You should see a list of words in the pop-up window. These are words BonziBUDDY will recognize. If you don’t see any words or you see the phrase "Speech input is not available for English," please review the information under the first question above. If you do see a list of words, please try the following:

    First, right-click on Bonzi and select My Options, then select Settings.  When the settings window opens, click on the "Advanced" button in the lower left hand corner of the window. Under the Output tab you should see checks in front of 1) Display spoken output in word balloon using, 2) Play spoken audio and 3) Play character sound effects. If you don’t have a check in front of these options, please check them and click the "Apply" button. Next, click on the Speech Input tab and make sure you have checks in front of 1) Characters listen for input, 2) Display listening tips and 3) Play tone when you can speak.  If you checked any of these options, make sure that you click Apply.

    If everything is okay with your Advanced Options, right-click on Bonzi and choose Goodbye.  Then, click on Start > Settings > Control Panel, double click on the Speech icon, highlight the Microsoft Speech Recognition Engine 4.0 (English) and press the Adjust Microphone Settings button. Please make sure you've gone through each screen here before performing the training.  (Note: Please make sure your volume levels are at least at the half level.) 

Next, please go through the training.  To do this, click Start > Settings > Control Panel, double click on the Speech icon, highlight the Microsoft Speech Recognition Engine 4.0 (English) and press the Training button.

Anytime you want BonziBUDDY to listen, hold down your Scroll Lock key until a small window pops up under Bonzi that says, "Bonzi is listening."  Once you see this message, you may speak any of the commands listed in Bonzi's menu.

  1. When I click on the Training button, why doesn't anything happen?

    When this occurs it generally means the Speech Recognition engine wasn’t loaded properly. To fix this, please do the following:

    Please uninstall the Talking Add-On for BonziBUDDY.  To do this, please click Start > Settings > Control Panel, double click on the Add/Remove Programs icon and remove the Microsoft Speech Recognition Engine 4.0 (English).   If you don't see the Microsoft Speech Recognition Engine, please go to the next step.

    Please open My Computer > C: Drive > Program Files > BonziBUDDY. Once the BonziBUDDY folder is open, run the ACTCNC file by double clicking on it. This will reinstall Microsoft’s Speech Recognition software.  (If the ACTCNC file is not there, please redownload the Talking Add-On file (BB1SETUP.EXE) by clicking here.

    Once you've reinstalled the ACTCNC file or re-downloaded the Talking Add-On module, please give the training another shot. First please make sure that BonziBUDDY is closed by right-clicking on him and choosing Goodbye. Then click Start > Settings > Control Panel and double click on the Speech icon. Under the Speech Input option, please highlight the Microsoft Speech Recognition Engine 4.0(English) and press the Training button.

    If you still experience problems clicking on the Training button, please click Start > Settings > Control Panel, double click on the Multimedia Icon, click the Audio tab and toward the bottom, please make sure the "Use only preferred devices" box is checked. It is also important that you have a sound card selected under Preferred device. If it shows, "Use any available device," there could be a problem.  You might need to reinstall the drivers for your sound card using the disk or CD that came with the sound card or your computer.  Contact your computer or sound card manufacturer for more information and/or updated drivers.

  2. Will BonziBUDDY work with Netscape?

    BonziBUDDY only works with Internet Explorer.  To get a FREE copy of Internet Explorer, please Click here.

  3. What are the System Requirements for BonziBUDDY?

    16MB of RAM, 11MB of Free Disk Space (not including add-on modules)
    Windows 95, 98, NT 4.0, ME, 2000, or XP
    Sound Card and Speakers
    Microsoft Internet Explorer 4.02 or above (we recommend version 5.0 or higher)

  4. When I open the Calendar, why is my time in Military time (00:30 - 23:30)?

    If your calendar is running in Military time, you can change this by doing the following:  Please click Start, Settings, Control Panel, double click on the Regional Settings icon and select the Time tab.  Next, select the h:mm:ss tt option under the Time Style field.

  5. Why can't I access the Calendar?

    If you are unable to access the Calendar, you may be missing one of the necessary files needed to run the calendar.  You need to have the most recent version of the MFC42.DLL file.    Close all opened programs before you begin. Please rename the MFC42.DLL file in your C:\Windows\System directory to MFC42.HLD and then replace it with this one: Click here to download the most recent version. You may need to reboot your computer (shut it down and then restart it) for these changes to take effect.

  1. BonziBUDDY tries to connect to the Internet without my permission. What can I do?

    Please make sure you've got the latest version of BonziBUDDY by clicking here.

    If you have the latest version of BonziBUDDY and are still experiencing this problem, please click here to contact our Technical Support Center.

  2. I have upgraded to Microsoft Windows XP and I can no longer hear BonziBUDDY speak?

Windows XP uses a different version of a program called "Speech API" that is not compatible with the current version of BonziBUDDY.  To fix this problem, click on the link here for the SPCHAPI.EXE file and choose to Run the file from its current location.  After this is installed, restart your computer and try Bonzi again.
