Downloading

  1. My download was unsuccessful. My Internet provider cut me off. I do not have my software yet. What do I do now?

    Please fill-in the the "E-Mail Request Form."  We will verify your purchase and send the download instructions directly to your e-mailbox.

  2. I lost my original download. How can I get a replacement copy?

    Please fill-in the the "E-Mail Request Form."  We will verify your purchase and send the download instructions directly to your e-mailbox.

  3. Do I download to my diskette drive or my hard drive?

    You must download to your hard drive. The download will not work if you are trying to download to diskette.  The easiest thing to do is, when you see the Save As window, pull down the menu at the top of the window and select Desktop.  Then click on the Save button.  What this will do is save the file to your desktop where all of your other icons are when you start your computer.  Then, it's as easy as double clicking on the file with your left mouse button to start the installation of your module.

  4. While I am downloading BonziBUDDY, it tells me to insert Disk 2?

    Please make sure to download the program to your hard drive. If you need another download, please fill-in the the "E-Mail Request Form."  We will verify your purchase and send the download instructions directly to your e-mailbox.
