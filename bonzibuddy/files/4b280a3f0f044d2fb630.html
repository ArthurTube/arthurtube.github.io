Sending Voice E-Mail

  1. When I try to send a Voice E-Mail to an AOL address, I get the following error message: 35163 or 20162.

    Please add less-than "<"  and greater-than ">" symbols to the front and back of the email address in your "Address" box. For example: <example@aol.com>.

    If you don't have the most recent version, please  Click here.  This will take you to the updates page.

  2. I get the following error message when I try to send a Voice E-Mail: "Runtime error 20162/5035.0.0.Proxy.aol.com/duplicate helo/ehlo.

    3. Please redownload the program by clicking on this address and following the instructions: http://www.bonzi.com/support2.htm. 

  3. I send a message but it does not arrive.

    Please verify that your email address is entered correctly in your Voice E-Mail User Preferences. To do this, double click on the Voice E-Mail 4.0 icon on your desktop. From the File menu, select User Preferences. This will bring up the User Preferences you entered at the time of installation. Please verify that you have entered your full Internet email address correctly (eg. johndoe@aol.com). If your address is not entered correctly in this field, your mail will be undeliverable.

  4. I get a message saying my mail was undeliverable even though my Voice E-Mail was sent successfully.


    5. When sending a Voice E-Mail message, please verify that you have entered the recipient’s correct Internet email address in the Address field. Please check carefully for spelling to ensure successful delivery.

  5. When I try to send, the message starts to upload or locate the server and is not able to send fully.

    To remedy this, please be sure that you are signed on to your ISP (connected to the Internet) and that you have your browser open. Once you have done this, try again and you should be able to send.

    If that didn’t work, please make sure you have the latest version of Voice E-Mail.  The latest version is 4.0.9, build #8 created on 5/18/99.   To check your version, please double click on the Voice E-Mail icon on your desktop, click help and then about.  Toward the middle it should indicate the version and build number.

    If you don't have the most recent version, please Click here.  This will take you to the updates page.

  6. It says my message is going out through a Secondary Server. What does that mean?

When Voice E-Mail goes out via the ‘secondary server,’ it means there was a problem connecting with the destination ISP. Voice E-Mail then tried to send the voice e-mail from the servers at BONZI Software. For example, let's say that you sent a voice e-mail to a friend at 123@aol.com. When you click ‘Send,’ Voice E-Mail will try to send the Voice E-Mail by connecting to AOL's servers. If it can't connect, Voice E-Mail will try to send via a secondary route--BONZI Software's server.

Note: If you send a Voice E-Mail to multiple recipients, it will receive the secondary server message. All Voice E-Mail sent to multiple addresses will go through BONZI Software servers.
