No, BonziBUDDY is not "Spyware." The program does not collect any personal information about you or your surfing habits and it does not transmit any information about you to our company or to any other third party.

What is "Spyware"?

"Spyware" is software that secretly collects information about you without your knowledge and transmits the information to advertisers or other third parties. BonziBUDDY does not, nor has it ever, secretly collected or transmitted any information about you to our company or to any other third party.

 

What caused some users to think BonziBUDDY was "spyware"?

In the past, BonziBUDDY had an "artificial intelligence" feature that saved your keyword searches (on your computer) so that BonziBUDDY could make occasional suggestions about other web sites that might be of interest to you. It is important to note, however, this information never left your computer and BonziBUDDY never transmitted any information about you to our company or to any other third party. Nor was this information ever used for promotional or advertising purposes; it was only used to make the BonziBUDDY feel more "real" and "intelligent" by giving him the ability to make useful and helpful suggestions from time to time, thereby enhancing the user's enjoyment and understanding of the Internet. Regardless, the feature was removed from the program to end further confusion.
