Aperialblocks prime-family copy fix — 25 October 2026

Fixes the case where a multiplier build could appear unchanged from prime ×1.

Rules:
- Prime-family Build now generates the family by default.
- The exact original counted prime mask is always used in Auto/Original mode.
- Alternate masks are used only when the user explicitly selects Recovered alternate profile.
- Multiplier 2 creates 2 complete copies; multiplier 3 creates 3 complete copies; and so on.
- Every copy preserves all occupied and empty positions of the source-prime outline.
- Group width changes placement of complete copies only.
- The optional Keep complete source product checkbox remains available when a supplied product sprite should take priority.

Test example: Prime 197, Multiplier 2 => 394 with two complete copies of the exact 197 mask.
