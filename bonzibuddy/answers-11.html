Answers:

  1. Can I talk to BonziBUDDY?

    With the FREE version of BonziBUDDY, he can only speak to you.  If you'd like to be able to speak to him, we have a Talking Add-On Module that will give you this option.  Just click on Bonzi to open his menu and choose "Check for new add-ons."

    If you don't have the BonziBUDDY "Talking Add-On Module" and you click the "Talk to Me" option, a Voice Commands window will pop up.  In the window you will see, "Speech input is not available in English".  If you don't have the BonziBUDDY Talking Module, you will not be able to talk to your BonziBUDDY.

  2. Why can't I hear BonziBUDDY talk to me?

    Please try downloading BonziBUDDY again at http://www.bonzi.com/BonziBUDDY/BonziBUDDYFREE.asp .

    If you still can't hear BonziBUDDY, please check to make sure your "Use only Preferred Devices" box is checked.  To check, please click Start, Settings, Control Panel, and then double click on the Multimedia option.  Next, click on the Audio tab and make sure the "Use only Preferred Devices" box is checked.

    Please also make sure your settings are correct.   Please select "Options" from Bonzi's menu. Then click the "Advanced" button in the bottom left-hand corner of the screen. Under the Output tab you should see checks in front of 1) Display spoken output in word balloon using, 2) Play spoken audio and 3) Play character sound effects. If you don’t have a check in front of these options, please check them and click the "Apply" button. Next, click on the Speech Input tab and make sure you have checks in front of 1) Character listen for input, 2) Display listen tips and 3) Play tone when you can speak.

    If these suggestions don't fix the problem (or if BonziBUDDY's voice isn't clear and audible), you may need to re-install your sound card drivers or the software that came with your sound card.

  3. When I click on the "Talk to Me" option, it reads "Speech Input is not available for English."  What does that mean?

    With the FREE version of BonziBUDDY, he can only speak to you.  If you'd like to be able to speak to him, we have a Talking Add-On Module that will give you this option.  Just click on Bonzi to open his menu screen and choose, "Check for new add-ons."

    If you have the "Talking Add-On Module" and click the "Talk to Me," you should see all of the words BonziBUDDY will recognize when you talk to him.

    If you don't have the "Talking Add-On Module" and you click on the "Talk to Me" option, a Voice Commands window will pop-up.  In the window you will see, "Speech input is not available in English".  Since you don't have the Add-On module, you won't be able to talk with BonziBUDDY.

  4. What are the system requirements for BonziBUDDY?

    16MB of RAM, 11MB of Free Disk Space
    Windows 95, 98 or NT 4.0 (English version required).
    Sound Card
    Microsoft IE (Internet Explorer) 5.0 or above

  5. What are the best video settings to use with BonziBUDDY?

    Please right-click on your desktop and choose Properties. Click on the "Settings" tab. (You can also go to Start > Settings > Control Panel > Display > Settings.)     When running BonziBUDDY, we recommend the following video settings:

    A screen resolution of 800 x 600 or higher.
    A color setting of 8 bit or higher.
    Small fonts. (The Fonts option can be found by clicking the "Advanced" button in the bottom right-hand corner of the Settings window.)

  6. When I open the Calendar, why is my time in Military time (00:30 - 23:30)?

    If your calendar is running in Military time, you can change this by doing the following.  Please click Start, Settings, Control Panel, double click on the Regional Settings icon and select the Time tab.  Next, select the h:mm:ss tt option under the Time Style field.

  7. Why can't I access the Calendar?

    If you are unable to access the Calendar, you may be missing one of the necessary files needed to run the calendar.  You need to have the most recent version of the MFC42.DLL file. Please download MFC40RT.EXE to a folder on your hard drive and then double click on the file to run it.  It will update your MFC42.DLL file for you and fix this problem.

  1. How can I uninstall BonziBUDDY?

It is a six-step process to remove BonziBUDDY.  Please follow these instructions carefully.

  • Click on Bonzi to open his menu screen and choose "Goodbye."
  • Go to Start > Programs > BonziBUDDY > Uninstall BonziBUDDY. Allow the uninstall program to finish.
  • If you would like to change your home page, please click on Start > Settings > Control Panel. Choose Internet Options.  Under the "General" tab, the first option should be entitled "Home Page."  Enter the web site you would like to have as your home page and click "OK."
  • If you would like to remove the BonziBUDDY Web Compass, go to Start > Settings > Control Panel > Add/Remove Programs, highlight the BonziBUDDY Web Compass, and click on the Add/Remove button.
  • Restart your computer to complete the uninstall.

  1. What is BonziMAIL?

BonziMAIL is the newest feature of BonziBUDDY.  BonziMAIL allows you to send emails to anyone on the Internet using Bonzi himself to convey the message!  You just type the address you are sending to and a subject for the message.  Then write your message and drag and drop Bonzi's animations into the message.  Wherever an animation is placed in the message, Bonzi will display that animation (like a hug or blowing a kiss) when he reaches that point in the message.  When the recipient gets your message, all they do is open the BonziBUDDY attachment to the Email and Bonzi will read the message, with animations, to them!  It's the greatest new feature, give it a try!

  1. I send messages using BonziMAIL and Bonzi tells me that they have been delivered, but the people I send them to never receive them.

If the people you send BonziMAIL messages to are not receiving your messages, but Bonzi notifies you that he is delivering them, try looking at the following.

Select "Options" from BonziBUDDY's menu.  On the Email Account tab, please note what you have entered as your Email Address. If you are connecting to America Online for your Internet access, make sure that this is set to your AOL.com email address (for instance, johnbrown@aol.com). This will increase the success you have with BonziMAIL messages due to the way that AOL accepts and processes email messages. If you are using a web-based email system such as Hotmail, Yahoo, Excite, or other email boxes that are accessed through a web browser, you might want to change this to  the email address that you were given by the provider of your Internet access. For example, if your Internet access is through a national provider like Earthlink or MindSpring, use your earthlink.com or mindspring.com email address here. This will also increase the amount of success you have with BonziMAIL.

  1. I create animated email messages and click on 'send' but BONZI never delivers the message.

If BONZI is not responding to the 'send' button by trying to deliver the message, you may need to install a newer update of BonziBUDDY to fix this. Look at the guidelines in the answer to the issue above and make sure that you are following them. You may be able to fix the problem by removing BonziBUDDY. To uninstall BonziBuddy, please go to Start > Programs > BonziBUDDY and remove any Add-on  modules you may have installed.  Then please go to Start > Settings > Control Panel > Add and Remove Programs and remove BonziBUDDY. Then please restart your computer and reinstall BonziBUDDY again at http://www.bonzi.com. 

  1. I get an error that says the EMSMTP.DLL file is missing or wasn't downloaded correctly.

This problem is usually caused when BonziBUDDY was installed while certain virus protect or crash guard programs were running. It is always good to disable programs like Norton Anti-Virus, Norton Crash Guard, or McAfee Virus Scan when you install any kind of software. If you are getting the above error, reinstall the BonziBUDDY files with these kinds of programs disabled.

First, uninstall BonziBUDDY. To uninstall BonziBuddy, please go to Start > Programs > BonziBUDDY and remove any Add-on  modules you may have installed.  Then please go to Start > Settings > Control Panel > Add and Remove Programs and remove BonziBUDDY.  Once he is gone, restart your computer and when it is restarted, disable the virus scan or crash guard program. You can usually do this by right-clicking on the system tray icon for the program next to your clock in the lower right-hand corner of your screen and choosing "exit" or "disable." Once the program is disabled, log on to the Internet and go to http://www.bonzi.com and install the BonziBUDDY again.

IMPORTANT: Make sure that you don't check your Email or run any email file attachments while your virus scanner is not running, as this increases your likelihood of getting emailed a computer virus.

Once the install of BonziBUDDY is complete, restart your computer to finalize the BonziBUDDY install and turn your virus scanner or crash guard program back on. This should fix the problem you were having with BonziMAIL. Go back up to the first issue above and make sure that your set up is following the guidelines listed there.

  1. I need to change the email address I use for BonziMAIL.

You can change your email address in BONZIMail. Just select "Options" from BonziBUDDY's menu (in the lower left-hand corner of the screen). Click on the Email Account tab.  You'll see a setting there for Email Address.  Just change it there and the change will be noticed in BonziMAIL.

  1. The "Import Addresses" option in BonziMAIL isn't working.

At this time, the Import Addresses option in BonziMAIL will only work with Outlook address books. It won't work with Outlook Express or any other address book format. 

  1. How can I save the BonziMAIL messages that I send?

BonziMAIL does not have a sent mail folder. If you need to save a copy of the BonziMAIL messages that you send, just CC (carbon copy) the messages to your own Email address when you send them.  That way you'll be able to store them in your regular email program.

  1. I'm using AOL as my ISP. How can I open my BonziMAIL messages?

AOL requires you to download your received BonziMail messages prior to playing them, and defaults to your C:\America Online\Download folder.

When you receive your BonziMAIL, please click the Download Now or Download File button at the bottom of the screen. Save the download to your download folder.  (C:\America Online\download) . 

After the file has downloaded to your computer, please click on the Start button, click on Run, and click on the Browse button in the Run Window.  Next, please click the dropdown arrow to the right of the Look In field and then select the C: drive.  Now, you should see some folders below.  Please double click on the appropriate AOL folder – America Online folder.  

Please double click on the Download folder.  At this point, you should see the BonziMAIL (.BBM) file you downloaded to your computer.  If not, please be sure that the File Type field at the bottom of that window is set to “All Files.”  Double click on the BonziMAIL file, and it should open BonziBUDDY. If BonziBUDDY is installed on your machine,  he should read you the message.

  1. How do I remove the BonziBUDDY Web Compass?

The BonziBUDDY Web Compass is a free feature and is independent of BonziBUDDY, so you may keep it on your Internet Explorer toolbar if you would like. It does not slow down your browsing speed. If you want to remove it, just go to Start > Settings > Control Panel > Add/Remove Programs and uninstall the BonziBUDDY Web Compass from there.
