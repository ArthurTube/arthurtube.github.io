NAM MOS Meteograms


Current meteogram for Philadelphia

A meteogram is a 60 hour time cross-section of NAM MOS data for a specific surface reporting station. The data plotted include temperatures, winds, pressure, clouds and present weather. These plots are updated once every 12 hours at around 12:45 EST. More Information
