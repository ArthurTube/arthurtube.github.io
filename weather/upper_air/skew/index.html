Upper Air Sounding Plots


Current SkewT from Washington DC

This directory contains vertical sounding plots from individual balloons. These are plots of temperature, dewpoint and winds with height. The soundings cover most of the available sites in the US and parts of southern Canada. There is also a listing of sounding data (in text form) including most major indices. These data update once every 12 hours at around 10:45 EST.
