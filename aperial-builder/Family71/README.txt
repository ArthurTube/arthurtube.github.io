71 FAMILY BUILDER

Open Family71_Builder.html after extracting the whole ZIP. No reference-bank import is required.

Supplied examples: 71, 142, 213, 284. The exact occupied square masks were counted from the supplied SVG. The original 1-300 band costumes/scripts are available separately as animation references and .sprite3 files. They are distinct sources; compare them with the supplied drawing.

Later multipliers 5-100 are generated shape drafts, not official character artwork. Each base cell becomes a width-by-height group of square cells. The original prime mask and the alternative 71-cell profile recovered from x2/x3 are selectable. Colours are editable draft choices. No invented face, limbs, glow or music is added. You can import complete SVG animation poses to supply those.

Exports: SVG; .sprite3; .sb3. New generated bodies are static unless SVG animation poses are imported. Original animation sprites retain source scripts with a new standalone green-flag entry point; audio is the compact reference-bank Ogg copy. Native Scratch playback still needs testing.
