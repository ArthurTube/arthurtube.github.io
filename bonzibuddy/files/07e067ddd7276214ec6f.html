Answers:

  1. How can I set up BonziBUDDY to read my e-mail?

    Once you have the BonziBUDDY E-Mail Reader installed, you will need to setup your E-Mail account and server information. This information will allow BonziBUDDY to check your e-mail and read it for you. Please right-click on BonziBUDDY, choose My Options, and click on Settings.  Then  click the E-mail Account tab. Enter the name or the IP address of your Internet Mail Server. This is also known as your POP 3 Mail Server and it is set in the properties of your regular e-mail client (ie: Outlook Express, Eudora, Netscape Messenger). Then enter your Account Name and Password.  Your Account Name is usually the same as the username or login that you use when you first sign on to your Internet Service Provider (ISP).

    If you're unsure about this information, it can easily be obtained from your ISP. Once you have entered this information, go ahead and click the 'Test' button. BonziBUDDY will make sure you have everything setup properly. If you would like, you can click on BonziBUDDY's 'Interval' tab, and specify how often you would like him to check for new E-Mail messages when you are online together.

  2. How do I know what my External Mail server name/address is?

    You can configure BonziBUDDY Mail to read messages from any e-mail account to which you have POP3 access. An account with POP3 access is one that you are able to access through an external email client such as Outlook Express, Eudora, or Netscape Messenger.

    In order to configure an external mail server, you must enter your POP3 Mail Server's name, your user name for that POP3 account, and the password for that account. Unfortunately, Bonzi Software technical support does not know and cannot provide you with any of this information. For further assistance in tracking down this information, you will need to contact your Internet Service Provider or Network Administrator.

  3. I am using the CS2000 version of Compuserve 2000. Will I be able to use the Mail Reader?

    We are sorry; Compuserve members using the CS2000 version of Compuserve will not be able to use the E-Mail Reader. CS2000 does not support POP3 mail protocols.

  4. I can't get BonziBUDDY Mail Reader to work with AOL?

    Currently, AOL does NOT support POP3 access. The E-Mail Reader will only work with email accounts that have been set up for POP3 access.  AOL does not allow third-party mail programs like BonziBUDDY to read messages from their mail servers.

  5. I can't get BonziBUDDY Mail Reader to work with Lycos, Hotmail, Juno, Rocketmail, Etc?

Currently, the BonziBUDDY E-Mail Reader cannot be configured to retrieve messages from web-based email accounts such as Lycos, Rocketmail, Hotmail, Juno, etc. The E-Mail Reader will only work if your e-mail account has been set up for POP3 access.  If you access your e-mail from a web page it is unlikely that you have a POP3 server to use for the E-Mail Reader.  The only exception that we know of is Yahoo! Mail, which allows POP3 access to free e-mail accounts if you sign up for the Yahoo! Delivers service.  Contact Yahoo help from the main page of your Yahoo mail box to learn more about Yahoo! Delivers.  To sign up for Yahoo! Delivers:

  • Sign into your Yahoo! Mail account.
  • Click on "Options" on the left-hand navigation bar.
  • Click on "Yahoo! Delivers" on the Options Page.
  • Click "Yes" under Sign Up.
  • Fill out the form and click the "Finished" button.

 

  1. I entered my Mail Server information, but it doesn't seem to be working.

    Please verify that you have entered the correct information. You may need to double-check the information with your ISP. Your mail server information may be case-sensitive. In addition, your Mail Server data may be correct, but you may have entered an incorrect account name (username) or password. Please note: you don't need to add the extension of your email address when you type in your username. You only need to type in the beginning of your email address. For example, if your email address is example@mymail.com, you only need to enter the first part, in this case: example into the Account Name field.

  2. I am receiving this error message: "This message was received with a demo copy of the Easy Mail POP3 Object."

    The latest version of BonziBUDDY should solve this problem. Please go to Bonzi.com and click on the link to run the BonziBUDDY free download in the Software and Services section near the bottom of the page.  This will update your BonziBUDDY to the newest version, but will not interfere with any modules you have installed.

  3. When I try to test my Mail Server information, the following error message appears: "Agent 429 Active X."

    Please click Start and then RUN. Please enter: "regsvr32 empop3.dll" without the quote marks.  Click the RUN button. If it tells you that the DLL file is registered, then try using the Mail Reader again. 

    If this doesn't return a successful response, please click here to contact our Technical Support Center. Please include your customer number in your email message.

  4. I receive a new e-mail, but when I ask BonziBUDDY to check my e-mail, he tells me there is nothing new.

First, please verify that your Interval setting is set to 1 minute. You can do this by right-clicking on Bonzi, choosing My Options, and clicking on Settings. Please click on "E-Mail Interval.". Please set the Interval to 1 minute. Please note: if your e-mail is pulled into your INBOX in your normal e-mail program before BonziBUDDY sees it on the Mail Server, he will not alert you of any new e-mail. If this is occurring, please send yourself an e-mail and then exit your e-mail service. BonziBUDDY should then see the message when it arrives and let you know it is there. Anytime BonziBUDDY catches a new email, he will prompt you and ask if he can read it for you. No matter what, the email will always go into your inbox so you can review and respond to it later, as you normally do in your e-mail program.
  1. I am using Outlook Express as my email program. When I receive new email messages, BonziBUDDY tells me I don't have any.

In order to use the Mail Reader for BonziBUDDY with Outlook Express, you will need to disable the Option to “Check for mail automatically." You can do this by going to the Tools menu in Outlook Express. Select Options and under the Send/Receive Messages section, uncheck the Check Mail automatically option.

  1. Can the BonziBUDDY Mail Reader monitor more than one e-mail address at a time?

No. We're sorry, at this time the BonziBUDDY E-mail Reader can only monitor one e-mail address at a time.

  1. Can I stop BonziBUDDY when he starts telling me about a new e-mail?

Yes. Once BonziBUDDY starts telling you about a new e-mail, you can stop him by double-clicking on him.

  1. When I ask BonziBUDDY to check my email messages, he tells me that I have 56, no matter how many I actually have.

This problem has been fixed and is included in the new update for BonziBUDDY.  Please go to Bonzi.com and click on the link to run the BonziBUDDY free download in the Software and Services section near the bottom of the page.  This will update your BonziBUDDY to the newest version, but will not interfere with any modules you have installed.
