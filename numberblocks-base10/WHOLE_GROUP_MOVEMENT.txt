WHOLE PLACE-VALUE GROUP MOVEMENT — COMPLETE FEATURE
The new Move whole place-value groups panel supports 1–1,000,000,000,000.
Choose Rectangle and Rectangle body draft, select a group, then use Move earlier/later or beginning/end. The selected group is outlined blue in its group-order preview. Groups may also be selected by clicking their visible region.
Groups include ones, tens, hundreds, thousands, ten-thousands, hundred-thousands, millions, ten-millions, hundred-millions, billions, ten-billions, hundred-billions and a trillion.
Each group keeps its exact value, colour family and border styling. Moving an unequal-sized group reflows the remaining groups along rectangle rows. This keeps the rectangle completely filled without overlaps or gaps; it does not translate a fixed polygon into overlapping space.
For instance, 2001 has two groups of 1000 and one group of 1; move the 1 to the beginning to place it before the thousands. A number that consists of only one complete group has no other group to move past.
Undo group move restores the preceding order. Reset group order returns to the original reference/default arrangement. Moving a whole group clears individual block swaps for that number, because the group order changes the base arrangement those swaps refer to.
Save block and group arrangements uses the existing arrangement JSON download. Version 2 files contain both kinds of edits; old version 1 block-only files still load. Imports validate all entries before applying changes.
Group moves affect generated rectangle previews, SVG, sprite3, SB3, and counting-glow exports. Supplied original costumes are unchanged. Original 3D reference-group views keep their canonical geometry/order; the new movement controls operate in rectangle mode.
Individual block editing remains available up to 5100, while whole-group editing uses at most 108 decimal groups and supports the full trillion range without expanding each unit cell.
Changes are kept in the open page until the arrangements JSON is saved; reload it in a future session.
