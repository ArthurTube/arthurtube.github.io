Answers:

  1. How do I use the BonziBUDDY© Games Modules?

Just right-click on Bonzi's tummy to bring up the menu. Then click on Play Games.  This will open a side menu where you can choose a game you want to play!  Bonzi can play three games if you've purchased the BonziBUDDY Gold Membership: Beach Checkers, Jungle Jigsaw, and Solitaire.

  1. My download failed and I did not get my product.

Sometimes downloads fail; we just have to live with that as one of the nuances of the Internet.  If your download attempt did not complete successfully, please visit our Email Request page to get another copy.  You will need your credit card number to fill out the form on this page. Please do not buy another copy of the Gold Membership as it will result in your being charged another fee for the purchase.

  1. How do I import my own picture into Bonzi's Jungle Jigsaw game?

Just open the game. When you see the picture selection screen open, where you pick which image you want to unscramble, just click on the "Open" button in the lower left corner of the window. This will open a Windows explorer screen. Navigate to the picture file that you want to use as a puzzle, click on it to highlight it, and then click on the Open button. The image will be imported right into the game for you!

  1. I am trying to move one of my pieces on the checkers board but it will not let me move. What am I doing wrong?

    In Bonzi's checkers game, there is a rule that if there is a jump move available to you, you MUST make that jump. You'll notice that Bonzi will set you up to make a single jump just so that he can do a double jump to outsmart you! If you can't move a piece you're trying to move, look around and you'll probably find a jump move that you're being forced to make.

  1. My Games Modules aren't working. What should I do?

Most of the problems that happen with this kind of add-on module happen because of a corrupted download file or a bad installation attempt.  If you are having problems with the games, please try downloading and installing the module again.
