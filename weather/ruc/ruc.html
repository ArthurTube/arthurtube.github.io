RUC Model Termination Notice


As of 12:00 UTC on 1 May, 2012 the RUC model has been replaced by the new RAP (Rapid Refresh) Model. Please update your bookmarks and links to their new locations. The new RAP products are located at http://weather.unisys.com/rap.


More information about this new RAP model can be found at http://rapidrefresh.noaa.gov.
