Check for New Add-Ons

Clicking on this item will take you to the BonziBUDDY Add-On Module page where you can see which add-on modules you currently have and which ones are available for purchase.

My Home

Clicking on this option will take you to Bonzi's home page!  From there you can personalize your page to show you your local weather and learn about all of the day's top headlines.

Search

Are you looking for something in particular?  You can search for keywords, such as cars, new software or a specific company name.  Type in the word or words you would like to find and click on the picture of Bonzi searching the world.  BonziBUDDY will help bring back the perfect match.  Even after you stop searching, BonziBUDDY will work in the background looking for other sites you haven’t visited. He will randomly ask you if you’d like to visit these new sites.

Browse

You can visit any website you wish by clicking BonziBUDDY's "Browse" command and entering the web site's address.  There is no need to enter the http:// part of the address.  For example, if you type in www.bonzi.com - BonziBUDDY will immediately take you to that website.

Check for Breaking News

Click here to have BonziBUDDY look for the most recent news updates from your list of interests.  To edit your list of news interests, right-click on Bonzi, go to My Options, and choose Registration.

Check for Virus Alerts

This item will tell BonziBUDDY to look at his news sources for new virus alerts that are circulation on the Internet.

Speak

Click here to open a window that will allow you type in anything that you want Bonzi to say.  When you're done typing and click on the button, Bonzi will say whatever you typed.  Remember to keep it clean!

Tell a Joke

Click here to ask Bonzi to tell you one of his many jokes.

Tell an Amazing Fact

Click this item to ask Bonzi to tell you one of his many amazing facts.

Sing a Song

This selection will open the BonziBUDDY Songs module if the module has already been installed.  There are many songs that Bonzi can sing for you.  Just pick the song you want to hear him sing!

Play Games

Casino Games, Sportsbook, and Head to Head (Tournament)

These links take you to the Games section of the Bonzi.com home page.  Play games with other people online at the time, try your hand at some gambling, or take a shot at placing a bet on your favorite team.  Just choose the appropriate link!

Bonzi's Beach Checkers, Bonzi's Jungle Jigsaw, and Bonzi's Solitaire

These items link to Bonzi's game modules.  If you have purchased any of the games that you can play with BonziBUDDY, you can open them here. Just click on the name of the game that you want to play!  With the proper modules installed you can play Checkers, Solitaire, or Jungle Jigsaw.

Read Stories

BonziBUDDY has four children's' stories that he can read with you.  His stories are Bonzi and the Polizoof, Bonzi and the Treasure Chest, Bonzi and the Internet, and Bonzi and the Alphanet.  If you'd like to read a story, just choose the right story name from the sub-menu.  Bonzi and the Internet is free, so choose that one if you would like to see what Bonzi's stories are all about.

E-Mail

New Message:
Use this item to open a new BonziMAIL message.  BonziMAIL will send animated e-mail messages to anyone on the Internet and, if they have a BonziBUDDY, Bonzi will read the message with animations.  Messages are fully customizable!  It's very easy to do, give it a try!

Check Our Mailbox:
If you've got the E-Mail Reader Add-On installed, BonziBUDDY will check for new e-mail messages when you choose this option. If you have a new e-mail message, Bonzi will let you know who sent the e-mail and what the subject is, and then prompt you to read the entire e-mail.

My Voice Commands

This is where to go to see the commands that can be spoken to operate Bonzi's very popular Talking Module.  The Talking Module will allow you to speak commands to Bonzi and he will understand and do what you ask!  With the Talking Module installed, Bonzi becomes a truly interactive friend.  Just click on this menu item to see the commands that Bonzi understands.

My Calendar

Keep track of all your appointments and things to do by entering them into the BonziBUDDY Calendar.  To do this, choose the "Calendar" option and then double click the day you would like to schedule something.  Next, type in the appointment and the time of the appointment.  You also have the ability to prompt BonziBUDDY to remind you of the appointment at a specific time.  (BonziBUDDY needs to be running on your computer to remind you of your appointments/tasks).

Download Files

This menu item opens the BonziBUDDY Download Manager.  Use the Download Manager to keep track of all of the files that you download from the Internet.  Just integrate the download manager with Internet Explorer and any time you start to download a file, Bonzi will take over and manage the file download for you!

Play Video and Audio

Use this menu item to open the BonziBUDDY Entertainment Center.  The Entertainment Center can play audio and video clips that you download or stream from the Internet.

Protect Yourself on the Internet

Click this item to learn more about how to protect yourself from Internet hackers while your computer is connected to the Internet.  At any time you're connected to the Internet, someone can sneak into your computer without you even knowing it!  Learn how to stop these attackers dead in their tracks!

Speed Up Your Internet Connection

Click here to learn about our exciting InternetBOOST program.  InternetBOOST will make your Internet connection (using Windows 95 or 98 and a modem) more efficient, resulting in decreased download times for web pages and files that you download from the Internet.  It can make your connection up to 200% faster, so check it out!

Share Me with Friends

Do you love BonziBUDDY and think that your friends will love him too?  Just click on the item labeled "Share Me with Friends" and enter up to three e-mail addresses.  When you've entered the addresses, just click on the picture of Bonzi!  Within minutes your friends will receive a BonziBUDDY e-mail notification telling them exactly "what", "where", and "how" to download BonziBUDDY for FREE!

Update Me

To make sure you have the latest version of BonziBUDDY running, simply click on this option.  If BonziBUDDY finds that you don't have the latest version, he will let you know.

My Options

Settings

Salutation:
How do you want BonziBUDDY to greet you?  After clicking the "Salutation" option, type in the name or title you want BonziBUDDY to call you.  The next time he refers to you, he'll call you by that name.  This is a great feature for multiple users.  PLEASE NOTE: BonziBUDDY does not pronounce words typed in all capital letters, he spells them out.  So, if BonziBUDDY is spelling out the letters in your name, rather than pronouncing your name, then type your name in lower case or capitalize only the first letter in your name.

Internet Connection:
In order for BonziBUDDY to best help you in your Internet travels together, he needs to know how you connect to the Internet. Please choose one of the options: dial-up modem, AOL, or always connected to the Internet.

E-Mail Account:
Once you have the BonziBUDDY E-Mail Reader installed, you will need to setup your E-Mail account and server information. This information will allow BonziBUDDY to check your e-mail and read it for you.  Please enter the name or the IP address of your Internet Mail Server. This is also known as your POP 3 Mail Server information. Then, enter your E-Mail logon User Name and Password. Click on the "Test" button to make sure everything is set up correctly.

Email Interval
This option will allow you to choose how often you would like BonziBUDDY to check your e-mail for you.

Download Manager
This option allows you to choose how you would like BonziBUDDY to manage your downloads. For more information about this option, please click here.

Entertainment Center
This BonziBUDDY option allows you to choose all of the file types that you want to be associated with BonziBUDDY's Entertainment Center. You can choose all, none or any number of files. You'll need to have BonziBUDDY's Entertainment Center installed in order to use this option. 

Sleep Mode
When you click on this option, you can choose how often BonziBUDDY will go into "Relax Mode." You can even keep him from ever going into his "Relax Mode."

Advanced Options:
Do you want to make BonziBUDDY talk faster or slower?   If you do, adjust the slider bar in BonziBUDDY's Advanced options window. There are also a few other options you have the ability to modify.  We recommend not altering these settings.

My Personality...

Choose this menu item if you would like to change the amount of Interaction your BonziBUDDY has with you.  Set your overall preference of how often you would like Bonzi to talk, then set your preference of which item you like best: jokes, facts, or personality items!

Support...

Click here if you're looking for Technical Support or Customer Service (help with your installation of BonziBUDDY).

Online Registration...

Click here if you want to update your registration information.  You can go here to change your e-mail address that we have on file, change your personal information, or set your news announcement preferences.

Quiet Mode - F9

If BonziBUDDY is in his "Sleep Mode," he'll sit with you on your desktop, but won't talk to you unless he needs to tell you about incoming emails, appointments, product enhancements or information about new sites. After he does this, he will still be in Sleep Mode (either sleeping or listening to some tunes). Pressing F9, as long as another program is not using that key, will put Bonzi in sleep mode.

Hide

When you choose this option, BonziBUDDY will take a break and swing away on his jungle vine. His banana icon will still appear in your system tray (in the bottom right-hand corner of your screen next to the clock) and he will still be able to remind you of calendar appointments and updates. If you'd like to interact with BonziBUDDY again, double-click on the icon in your system tray and BonziBUDDY will swing back onto your computer screen again.

Goodbye

Clicking on the "Goodbye" command will close  the BonziBUDDY program.  To get BonziBUDDY to return, simple double click on the BonziBUDDY icon on your desktop or click start > programs > BonziBUDDY and then select BonziBUDDY.
