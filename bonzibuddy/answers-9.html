Answers:

  1. How do I use the Songs Add-On Module?

Just right-click on Bonzi's tummy to bring up the menu. Then click on Sing a Song.  This will open the Songs Module screen where you can choose a song to play whenever you want to hear Bonzi's wonderful voice!  Bonzi can sing all of the songs in the Sing a Song window, just double click on the song you want him to sing or click once to highlight the name of the song and then click the Start button.

  1. My download failed and I did not get my product.

Sometimes downloads fail; we just have to live with that as one of the nuances of the Internet.  If your download attempt did not complete successfully, please visit our Email Request page to get another copy.  You will need your credit card number to fill out the form on this page.

  1. I asked Bonzi to start singing a song.  How do I get him to stop before he is finished?

All you have to do to stop Bonzi's serenade is to click on the Stop button at the bottom of the Songs window.

  1. My Songs Add-On Module isn't working.

Most of the problems that happen with this kind of add-on module happen because of a corrupted download file or a bad installation attempt.  If you are having problems with the Songs Add-On, please try downloading and installing the module again.

If the download page has been closed, you can visit our Email Request page to get another download.  This page requires your credit card number.
