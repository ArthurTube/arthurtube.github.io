Downloading

  1. My download was unsuccessful. My Internet provider cut me off. I do not have my software yet. What do I do now?

    Please fill-in the the "E-Mail Request Form."  We will verify your purchase and send the download instructions directly to your e-mailbox.

  2. I lost my original download, how can I get a replacement copy?

    Please fill-in the the "E-Mail Request Form."  We will verify your purchase and send the download instructions directly to your e-mailbox.

  3. Do I download to my diskette drive or my hard drive?

    You must download to your hard drive. The download will not work if you are trying to download to diskette.  The best thing to do is, when asked if you want to Save the file or Run the file, choose Save.  When the Save As window appears, pull down the menu at the top of the window and choose "Desktop."  By doing this, you will save the file to your Desktop with the other icons you see every time you first start up your computer.  When the file is done saving, you'll see it right there on your Desktop, and you can double click on the icon from there to start the program installation.  You must leave your Internet connection open while you are installing the module.
