Aperialblocks Band Builder
Arthur Tube release for the Numberblocks community

Website range: Bands 35 through 510 (numbers 341 through 5100).
Band 35 covers 341–350; Band 510 covers 5091–5100.

Aperial is described as a base-∞ (base infinity) / base-ω (base omega) numeral system, similar to Argam.
Official/canon Aperial numbers are by Aperial10080. Supplied official/canon references take priority; generated builder extensions and drafts must not be labelled official unless confirmed by the creator.
Official project: https://scratch.mit.edu/users/Aperial10080/
The builder keeps supplied/reference artwork ahead of generated drafts and includes the current prime-family, checkerboard, own-face, block-line, colour and shape correction logic from the uploaded builder package.
