Image and Map Archive

This is an archive of satellite images, surface maps and upper air data for the past couple of months.


Surface Maps
This is a composite map contain the following analyses: radar summary (color filled areas), surface data plot (composite station model), frontal locations (in various bold lines) and pressure contours (in thin blue lines). For More Information


Infrared Satellite Images
This image shows the emmision from the infrared portion of the solar spectrum. Light areas are cold (possible clouds) and dark areas are warm (and possibly clear). For More Information


Upper Air Charts
These images show upper air conditions with a 4 panel chart. These are derived from the ETA model initial gridded data. The top row of charts are for the 850 mb and 300 mb levels. The bottom row of charts are the surface and Relative Humidity charts. 850 mb, 300 mb, Surface, Relative Humidity


SST Charts
These images show Sea Surface Temperatures (SST) and anomalies. They are archived about once a week.
