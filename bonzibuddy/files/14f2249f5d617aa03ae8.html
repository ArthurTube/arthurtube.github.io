BonziBUDDY� Operation Issues

  1. How do I remove/uninstall the free version of BonziBUDDY?

It is a six-step process to remove BonziBUDDY.  Please follow these instructions carefully.

  • Click on Bonzi to open his menu screen and choose "Goodbye."
  • Go to Start > Programs > BonziBUDDY > Uninstall BonziBUDDY Bargain Hunting Tool.  Allow the uninstall program to finish. If you don't have a BonziBUDDY Bargain Hunting Tool, ignore this step.
  • Go to Start > Programs > BonziBUDDY > Uninstall BonziBUDDY.  Allow the uninstall program to finish.
  • If you would like to change your home page, please click on Start/Settings/Control Panel. Choose Internet Options.  Under the "General" tab, the first option should be entitled "Home Page."  Enter the web site you would like to have as your home page and click "OK."
  • Restart your computer.

Thank you for trying BonziBUDDY!

  1. I am getting an EMSMTP.DLL error when I try to send BonziMAIL messages?

    This problem is usually caused when BonziBUDDY was installed while certain virus protect or crash guard programs were running.  It is always good to disable programs like Norton Anti-Virus, Norton Crash Guard, or McAfee Virus Scan when you install any kind of software.  If you are getting this error, reinstall the BonziBUDDY files with these kinds of programs disabled.

    First, uninstall BonziBUDDY by going to Start > Programs > BonziBUDDY > Remove BonziBUDDY.  Once he is gone, restart your computer and when it is restarted, disable the scanning program.  You can usually do this by right-clicking on the system tray icon for the program next to your clock in the lower right-hand corner of your screen and choosing �exit� or �disable.�  Once the program is disabled, log on to the Internet and go to http://www.bonzi.com/bonzibuddy/bonzibuddyfree.asp and install the BonziBUDDY again.

    IMPORTANT: Make sure that you don�t check your Email or run any email file attachments while your virus scanner is not running, as this increases your likelihood of getting e-mailed a computer virus.

    Once the install of BonziBUDDY is complete, restart your computer to finalize the BonziBUDDY install and turn your virus scanner or crash guard program back on.  This should fix the problem you were having with BonziMAIL.  Please also read #1 above.

  1. Can I go back to using Peedy the Parrot as my BonziBUDDY?

    Unfortunately, there is no way for us to support two different characters for BonziBUDDY.  We are very sorry if you have become fond of Peedy, but we are working hard to make sure that everything Peedy can do, Bonzi can do better!  The new features and functionality that we have planned for your BonziBUDDY in the near future have helped us to make this decision.  Bonzi has already made a tremendous impact on us, we hope you'll let him do the same with you.

    All of the add-on modules that work with Peedy will work with Bonzi.  You do not need to modify anything that you do with Bonzi - everything works in exactly the same way as it did before your last upgrade.  You do not need to change any options, they will all be transferred over to your new BonziBUDDY and they will work exactly as they did before the upgrade.

  2. How can I open and play my Bonzi-Mail messages? Do my recipients need to have BonziBuddy installed?

    When receiving a message through BonziMAIL, just open the file attached to the message to start BonziBUDDY and have him read the message.  You may want to uncheck the box at the bottom of the Open/Save As window that says �Always ask for this type of file.�  By un-checking this box you will not be asked if you want to open the file or save it.  Just turn off that check box and choose �Open� and from then on the BonziMAIL messages will always be opened when you select them.

    BonziMAIL works fine on Web-based Email services like Yahoo, Hotmail, and Excite!  Just choose to �Open the message attachment from its current location� and BonziBuddy will read the message to you while performing the embedded animations.

    Your recipients will need to have the latest version of BonziBuddy installed on their computer in order to view your Bonzi-MAIL message. When your recipients receive their Bonzi-MAIL messages, they will also receive instructions for downloading the latest version of BonziBuddy.

  1. I can't share BonziBUDDY with my friends.  What is the problem?

    The "share with friends" feature of BonziBUDDY works the same way as BonziMAIL.  Please see the answer to the BonziMAIL issue in #1 above.  If you would like to send a note to your friends about BonziBUDDY using your own e-mail program, just send them a link to http://www.bonzi.com/bonzibuddy/bonzibuddyfree.asp.

  1. How can I stop BonziBUDDY from starting every time I turn on my computer?

    BonziBUDDY is placed in your Windows startup folder so that it will start every time you boot up your computer.  If you would like to stop this from happening, right-click on your Start button and choose Open.  Then double click on Programs and double click on Startup.  In Startup you will see a BonziBUDDY item.  Just right click on the Bonzi icon and choose Delete and this will stop BonziBUDDY from starting up automatically.

    After this is done, you can double click on the BonziBUDDY icon on your desktop or go to Start > Programs > BonziBUDDY to start Bonzi.

  1. How do I change the name that BonziBUDDY calls me when he is talking?

    To change the way BonziBUDDY greets you, please click right-click on BonziBUDDY and go to "My Options." Click on "Settings." Then please choose the "Salutation" option. If BonziBUDDY is having trouble pronouncing your name, please try using all lowercase letters or try spelling your name as it sounds.

  2. I can see Bonzi's word balloons, but I can't hear him speak.  What should I do?

Please be aware that this issue happens a lot when customers are using a non-English version of Windows.  BonziBUDDY is written to work on English language versions of Windows only.  On non-English versions of Windows, BonziBUDDY will operate but will have many glitches and bugs, such as this.  For this reason, we cannot support running BonziBUDDY on non-English versions of Windows.

If you are running an English version of Windows, go to Start > Settings > Control Panel and open your Multimedia settings.  For the "preferred device" you will have a certain item selected.  Try changing this value and applying the change, then check to see if BonziBUDDY is working or not.  If this has no effect, set the preferred device setting back to its original value.

You also might want to try going to Start > Settings > Control Panel > Add and Remove Programs and just opening the Windows Setup tab.  This has also been known to clear up this problem.

If all else fails, please remove and reinstall your BonziBUDDY.  Go to Start > Settings > Control Panel > Add and Remove Programs and remove BonziBUDDY.  Then visit us again at http://www.bonzi.com and reinstall the BonziBUDDY files.  This should fix the problem if all else fails.

If you continue to have this problem, please fill out our technical support form.

  1. I have removed BonziBUDDY but when I download files I get a "Failed to create OBJDM," error from the BonziBUDDY Download Manager.  How can I fix this?
This error is caused when a file failed to remove itself during the BonziBUDDY uninstall process. To fix, first Close any open Internet Explorer, AOL, or other web browser windows that you have running.  Then, open the 'My Computer' > C: Drive > 'Windows' > 'System' folder and look for two files named IEHelperMiddleMan. When you find these two files, delete them, and you will no longer get this error.  If Windows displays a message indicating that the files are in use and cannot be deleted, then complete the following 6 steps for removal:

Steps to Uninstall and Remove IEHelperMiddleMan Files

Step 1: Shut down or close any applications that you have running (most importantly, close all open Internet Explorer browser windows).
Step 2: Click 'Start' button in Windows, then click 'Run'.
Step 3: Type in the following command, exactly as it appears below, into the Run dialog window and click OK:

regsvr32 /u iehelpermiddleman.dll
Step 4: Windows will remove registry entries associated with IEHelperMiddleMan and will then display the message, "DLLUnregisterServer in iehelpermiddleman.dll succeeded".  Click OK on this message.
Step 5: Reboot your computer.  You should no longer receive the "Failed to Create ObjDM" error message.
Step 6: If you like, after rebooting, you may safely delete the two IEHelperMiddleMan files from your System or System32 folder, as they are only required for the BonziBUDDY Download Manager.

If you still receive this error after completing the above steps, please contact technical support.

  1. I can't see all of the commands in BonziBUDDY's menu screen.

    This problem is the effect of using certain video settings on some computers. If you are having this problem, please     right-click on your desktop and choose Properties. Click on the "Settings" tab. (You can also go to Start > Settings > Control Panel > Display > Settings.) We recommend the following video settings:

    A screen resolution of 800 x 600 or higher.
    A color setting of 8 bit or higher.
    Small fonts. (The Fonts option can be found by clicking the "Advanced" button in the bottom right-hand corner of the Settings window.)
