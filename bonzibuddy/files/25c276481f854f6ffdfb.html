Downloading

  1. My download was unsuccessful. My Internet provider cut me off. I do not have my software yet. What do I do now?

    Please fill-in the "E-Mail Request Form."  We will verify your purchase and send the download instructions directly to your e-mailbox.

  2. I lost my original download, how can I get a replacement copy?

    Please fill-in the "E-Mail Request Form."  We will verify your purchase and send the download instructions directly to your e-mailbox.

  3. Do I download to my diskette drive or my hard drive?

    You must download to your hard drive. The download will not work if you are trying to download to diskette.  When you get the Save As window, the best thing to do is to pull down the menu at the top of the window and select Desktop.  If you save the folder to your desktop, it will show up with all of your other icons that you see when you first start up your computer.  Then, once the file is done downloading, all you have to do is double click on its icon on the desktop to run the installation of the module for BonziBUDDY.
