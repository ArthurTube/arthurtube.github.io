SELECT AND MOVE INDIVIDUAL BLOCKS
Open a number, then click Edit blocks / shape above the preview. Click and drag one square; Shift-click adds or removes squares. Drag from empty space to select a group. Arrow keys/buttons move the selection; Select same colour selects a whole colour group. Rotate selection, Arrange rows, Undo, Redo and Reset are included. Total count and per-block colours are preserved; overlapping moves are rejected. The scrollable canvas renders only the visible area, so large bodies do not require a huge canvas bitmap.
Click Apply shape to draft, then use the normal Scratch export controls. This edits a separate captured first-pose body draft; the complete supplied source is unchanged. Body glow/shape poses are frozen in the edited copy, while chosen face/limb reference tracks remain available for review. Face/limb placement may need adjustment after major shape changes. Save shape JSON and load it in this editor after reopening the same number. Only flat, individually countable square bodies are supported; a complex uncountable source must first be opened as a counted draft or supplied mask.
