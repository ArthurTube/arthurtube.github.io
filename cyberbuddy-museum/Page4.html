Multiple Simultaneous Characters
Now double your fun!
With Versions 3 or 4 of CyberBuddy, there can be multiple characters on the screen at the same time, instead of a single character. This is especially interesting in Instant Messaging.
To use this feature, you must have the Premium version of CyberBuddy.
Click the "Character and Speech Options" button on the "General" tab of Cyberbuddy. Make sure you have "Use Multiple Characters" selected, then check the box marked "Simultaneous Charcacters".
