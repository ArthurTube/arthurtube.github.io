All numbers 0–5100 were scanned. The table lists the 82 power numbers from 36 onward. Supplied names take priority. Existing flat names that match a valid construction remain. Other forms are explicit rule-generated proposals, not official names. Prefix/suffix joining is still subject to source verification.

The calculator honours the requested base/exponent and reports alternative existing names separately. For example, 5^4 gives Kield as a proposal, while the existing 625 name Pentund remains available. Canonical/user corrections such as 1296 Exeld override proposals.
