ECMWF Model Forecasts


Current ECMWF SL Pressure/500mb Height Forecast

NOTE: These are products that aren't considered "Additional Data" under resolution 40.

This directory contains contour plots from the European Center for Medium range Weather Forecasting (ECMWF) forecast model. This model runs to 7 days and provides data at 24 hour intervals for sea level pressure, 850 mb winds and temperatures and 500 mb heights. These plots are generated once a day at 8:45 PM EST.


500 mb / SL Pressure Forecast Plots
This plot shows two parameters: 500 mb heights (in color contours) and sea level pressure (in black line contours).
