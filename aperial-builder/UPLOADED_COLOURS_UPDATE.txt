APERIAL COLOUR REFERENCE UPDATE — 25 October 2026

Open Aperial_Builder_UPDATED.html and use “Uploaded Aperial colours · 1–2447”.
The new panel can inspect a numbered source swatch and create a separate body-colour draft.

SOURCE
Aperial colors.sprite3 contains 25 sheets, 100 swatch positions per sheet, populated through 2447. All original sheet SVGs are included in Colour_References_1-2447. The original sprite is also included unchanged.

CHANGES
• 2447 numbered swatches are available to the builder.
• Generated rectangular bodies can use the supplied composite checkerboard motifs.
• Prime body drafts use uploaded colour pairs instead of outdated generated pairs. 295 prime-bank entries after 340 were compared with the upload; 128 pairs differed and were corrected. 60 generated prime centres were also corrected to the uploaded black centre. The complete comparison is included.
• Prime-bank SVGs, its CSV/catalog, its importable sprite3, and normal family rendering use the corrected pairs.
• 3 is yellow #fffe2f. 6 is purple #723bc2. 9 uses #919191 / #aaaaaa / #d1d1d1.
• Your finished 311–340 designs, individual block painting, own faces, and source references keep priority. No faces, limbs, sounds or source scripts were replaced.
• Native timing boundaries are retained. The body-colour layer applies source colour changes over the new rest palette; it does not invent new costume timing.

2448 AND BEYOND
The existing closest-factor builder can combine available factor swatches. These are drafts: the upload does not verify their final colour placement, shapes, animation or prime names. Primes without an uploaded swatch retain an explicitly unverified letter-colour draft.

LIMITS
A colour swatch is not a body silhouette or a counted block diagram. The 84×84 interior sample preserves flat swatch fills and visible motifs; applying it to a differently sized body can change pattern spacing. Original vector sheets remain available for exact visual comparison. Prime centre colours follow the uploaded white/black swatches; counted outlines are preserved rather than copying the swatch’s square outline.
The original 1–300 reference animation and sound coverage has not been expanded by this update. No native Scratch playback verification is claimed.
