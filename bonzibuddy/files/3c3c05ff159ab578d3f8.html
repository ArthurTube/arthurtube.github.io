Once you have the BonziBUDDY™ E-Mail Reader installed, you will need to set up your E-Mail account and server information. This information will allow BonziBUDDY to check your e-mail and read it for you. Please  choose "Options" from Bonzi's menu screen, then click on the E-Mail Account tab. Please enter the name or the IP address of your POP3 Internet Mail Server. Then enter your E-Mail logon name or username (in the Account Name field) and your password.

Almost all Internet service providers support standard POP3 e-mail clients like Bonzi's E-Mail Reader, but some do not.  Some providers that we know for sure do not support the E-Mail Reader are America Online, MSN, Juno, Lycos, and Hotmail. MSN's pop server will not work with the E-Mail Reader module because it uses a security feature called Secure Password Authentication that is not compatible with Bonzi's E-mail Reader module.

 The easiest way to find your ISP's POP3 server information is to look in your e-mail program.  If you're using Outlook Express, for example, just open Tools > Accounts and go to the Mail tab.  Enter the Properties screen for your mail account, and check the Servers tab for the proper information.  The BonziBUDDY E-mail Reader needs to know what your Incoming or POP3 server name is.  Transfer this server name in its entirety (exactly letter-for-letter) into the proper field in the BonziBUDDY E-Mail Reader options page.

All e-mail programs with the exception of web-page based e-mail browsers should have a similar "account properties" page that lists the name of the mail servers that store your mail.  Contact your Internet Service Provider if you need more information.
