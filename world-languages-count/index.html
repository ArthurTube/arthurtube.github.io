About the source and the graph

Historical entries were extracted from the user-provided text “List of languages by first written account.” Dates preceded by “c.” or described by centuries are approximate. Century-only entries are plotted at an approximate midpoint; ranges are represented by a single approximate year for navigation. The list is incomplete, and first attestation is not the date a language was created.

At AD 1000, the chart belongs to history and rises because the source documents additional languages by this period. That does not establish that the total number of spoken languages worldwide rose in AD 1000. The pre-writing period before 3100 BC is intentionally shown with zero documented source entries, not zero spoken languages.

Source supplied by user: List of languages by first written account. This project is a visualization of that supplied list, not an independently verified comprehensive linguistic database.
