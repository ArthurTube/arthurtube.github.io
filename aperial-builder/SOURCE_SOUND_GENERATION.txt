SOURCE-BASED SOUND LINK GENERATION

Open index.html after extracting every file. Find “Generate an editable sound link from factor references”. Enter a number, review its canonical factor pair and original links, then generate. Copy the resulting URL or download editable JSON.

2088 = 36 × 58: both source songs are available and a generated source-layer draft can be encoded. 1395 = 31 × 45: 31 has source audio but no embedded editor link. Paste an approved editable 31 source link to enable the complete combination. Missing sources are never silently replaced with invented melodies.

The generator retains patterns/instruments, rebases note numbers to retain concert pitches across different keys, loops factors to a common complete length and uses the first factor’s global mixer settings. This is a layer combination for review, not a claim to an official arrangement or final mix. Conflicting tempo/meter, active modulation and unsupported pitch ranges block generation. Original individual links remain available for comparison.

The vendor encoding engine is from https://jukeebox.github.io/beepbox_editor.min.js, with editor startup removed and engine exports retained; its original MIT notice is retained. It runs in a hidden separate document to isolate editor CSS.
