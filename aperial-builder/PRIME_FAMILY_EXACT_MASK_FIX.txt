Aperialblocks Band Builder — Exact Prime Family Mask Fix
October 25, 2026

Prime-family generation now repeats the exact counted source-prime mask.
The source prime is never stretched, refilled, or reshaped by the multiplier.
Empty positions/holes in the source outline remain empty.

Example only:
197 × 2 = 394 -> two unchanged copies of the 197 mask.
197 × 3 = 591 -> three unchanged copies of the 197 mask.

This rule applies to every counted prime family, not only 197.
Complete supplied/canonical product sprites still take priority.
Group width controls only how whole prime copies are arranged.
