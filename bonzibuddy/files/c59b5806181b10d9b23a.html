BonziBUDDY™ Entertainment Center

The BonziBUDDY Entertainment Center is an interface for playing online multimedia content.  It can be used to watch movie trailers, listen to radio stations, follow broadcast sporting events, and even get up-to-the-minute news delivered right to your desktop!  Please read on if you are interested in learning how to use the Entertainment Center interface.  You'll be watching streaming multimedia content in no time! 

How does the Entertainment Center work?

Any time you select a link to a multimedia file supported by the BonziBUDDY Entertainment Center, the program will open automatically.  When the Entertainment Center window opens, it will first load a part of the streaming media file into your computer's memory (this is called buffering).  While the media stream is buffering, you will see a status indication (in %) in the lower left corner of the browser window.  When it is done buffering part of the media stream it will begin playing in the Entertainment Center's main window. 

What do the various buttons on the Entertainment Center window mean and how do they work?  

Clicking on the button labeled with a question mark (?) in the upper left corner will initiate Bonzi's help script to tell you a little about how the Entertainment Center works.

Media Selection - The cassettes in the top left corner of the window labeled Home, Music, Radio, and Broadband will take you to the Microsoft® WindowsMedia.com web site (http://www.windowsmedia.com).

Home - Takes you to the home page of the Windows Media web site.

Music - Takes you to the Music section of the Windows Media site where you can watch videos and listen to music from hundreds of your favorite artists.

Radio - This button sends you to the Radio section of the Windows Media web site where you can listen to hundreds of Internet radio stations broadcasting 24 hours a day.  Just follow the links to the music genre you would like to listen to and the Entertainment Center will take care of the rest!

Broadband - For users with high-bandwidth connections (Cable, DSL, T1, etc.) the Broadband section contains links to multimedia content with higher video resolution, better quality, and all-around superior performance.  Many of the high-bandwidth multimedia streams can be played full screen with a fast computer!

 Forward (left arrow), Back (right arrow), and Help (?). – These buttons control the content in the main browser window.  If there is a web page displayed there, back and forward work just like they do in your regular web browser.  The Help button (?) launches the help script that is related to you by Bonzi and brings up the overlay you see in the image above to show you which actions are performed by which buttons.

Address Bar and Go to URL (Go). – This window works just like it does in your regular web browser.  If you know the URL of a page you want to visit, it can be typed in here, then just click on the "GO" button.

Volume (round button). – This button is connected to your Windows volume control and raises and lowers the volume that is played back by the media player.  Just grab the dot and drag it clockwise to increase and counter-clockwise to decrease volume.

Play Controls. - These buttons control the entertainment center the same way that the buttons on a VCR would work.  From left to right, they are Rewind, Stop, Pause, Play, and Fast Forward.

Open Media (...) – Click on this button to play a media file that you know the location of.  This could be in a folder on your hard drive (on your computer) or at a web site.  Just type the URL (http://...) or the location (c:\...) of the file (or click Browse) and when you click OK, the Entertainment Center will play your media file.
