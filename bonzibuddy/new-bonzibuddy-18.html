What is "BONZI and the Polizoof!" for BonziBUDDY?

The story, 'Bonzi and the Polizoof!' is about a baby gorilla named "BONZI."  The story begins with BONZI and his friend Spot, discovering a black box deep in the jungle. From there, the journey begins! BONZI and Spot travel the jungle sharing their new discovery with all their friends!

What is the "Storybook Reader" Module for BonziBUDDY?

The "Storybook Reader" add-on module is my software that allows you and I to read stories together. I've designed several settings with my storybook software:

  • I can read the entire story to you. (Recommended for ages 2 and above.)

  • We read together. (Recommended for ages 4 and above.)

  • You read to me. (Recommended for ages 5 to 6 and above.)

The "BONZI and the Polizoof!" storybook, includes my Interactive Storybook reader software -- FREE!  This will enable us to enjoy "BONZI and the Polizoof!" for years to come.  In addition, I am currently developing a series of books that will all work with my Interactive Storybook reader software!
