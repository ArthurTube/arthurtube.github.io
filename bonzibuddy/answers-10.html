BonziBUDDY™ Storybook Reader™ Technical Support

Can't find your question in the links below? Please contact Customer Support!

Does Your Question Involve:

  1. When I click on the Storybook icon, the following message appears: "error message box: 364 - Object was unloaded."
  2. When I click on the Storybook icon, the following message appears" "Error 91: Object variable or with Variable not set."
  3. I installed "BONZ and the Polizoof" but it's not working properly.
  4. How do I access the printing, coloring book, and treasure chest features of "Bonzi and the Treasure Chest?"

Answers:

  1. When I click on the Storybook icon, the following message appears: "error message box: 364 - Object was unloaded."

Please check to make sure you have the latest version of BonziBUDDY.  To check, please click on BonziBUDDY's 'Update Me' option.  You can also click here to go to our updates page. Then please install the Storybook Reader again, but make sure that BonziBUDDY is closed before you do. Then please reboot your computer (shut it down and then restart it).

  1. When I click on the Storybook icon, the following message appears" "Error 91: Object variable or with Variable not set."

Please check to make sure you have the latest version of BonziBUDDY.  To check, please click on BonziBUDDY's 'Update Me' option.  You can also click here to go to our updates page.

Please reboot your computer (shut it down and then restart it) once the update is complete.

If that doesn't work, please exit BonziBUDDY and then reinstall the Storybook Reader. 

Please restart your computer once the installation is complete.

  1. I installed the storybook but it's not working properly.

Please restart your computer. Also check to make sure you have the latest version of BonziBUDDY.  To check, please click on BonziBUDDY's 'Update Me' option.  You can also click here to go to our updates page.

  1. How do I access the printing, coloring book, and treasure chest features of "Bonzi and the Treasure Chest?"

When you open the Bonzi and the Treasure Chest storybook, you will see a button with three dots (...) on it in the lower right corner of the storybook window.  Click this button to go to the options screen.  On the options screen you will see a PRINT button.  Clicking on this button will give you the options to print the storybook in three modes: Normal, Coloring Book, or Treasure Chest.  Normal will print the pages you specify on your printer (in color if your printer has the capability).  Coloring Book mode will print an outline of your selected page(s) just like a coloring book.  Treasure Chest will print out a craft project for you to put together that will create a treasure chest with your name on it!
